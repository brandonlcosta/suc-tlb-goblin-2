# Audio Style Guide

## Priority

Audio is optional early.

Do not let audio block gameplay.

## Direction

The audio should support:
- downhill pressure
- heat stress
- breathing
- crew urgency
- river/log crossing feedback
- second aid-station relief
- PS1 atmosphere

## Early Sounds

Good first sounds:
- footstep loop
- gravel/dirt step noise
- heat warning beep
- hydration warning beep
- ice/water sound
- river splash
- log footstep / creak
- crew interaction blip
- second aid interaction blip
- finish sound
- collapse sound

## Terrain Feedback

Useful later cues:
- faster footstep cadence on steeper downhill
- strained breath on short uphill
- braking scrape or heavy step
- splash when entering water
- dull stumble sound on failed log or rough hit

Keep sounds short, readable, and low-budget. The audio should communicate state, not become a huge system.

## Ambience

Later ambience:
- wind
- insects
- distant canyon sound
- muffled crew at start
- faint second aid noise
- breathing intensifies with effort

## Music

Optional.

If added later:
- low drone
- retro synth pulse
- intensity rises with heat/final push

No licensed music.

No huge audio system early.
