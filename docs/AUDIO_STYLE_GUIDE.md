# Audio Style Guide

## Priority

Audio is optional early.

Do not let audio block gameplay.

## Direction

The audio should support:
- downhill pressure
- heat stress
- breathing
- crew urgency
- PS1 atmosphere

## Early Sounds

Good first sounds:
- footstep loop
- gravel/dirt step noise
- heat warning beep
- hydration warning beep
- ice/water sound
- crew interaction blip
- finish sound
- collapse sound

## Ambience

Later ambience:
- wind
- insects
- distant canyon sound
- muffled crew at start
- breathing intensifies with effort

## Music

Optional.

If added later:
- low drone
- retro synth pulse
- intensity rises with heat/final push

No licensed music.

No huge audio system early.
